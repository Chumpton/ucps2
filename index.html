  • ✅ Offers a living clock system where the universe itself is a measurable cycle within the greater multiversal flow.
  • ✅ Uses fractal gears to visualize the rotational relationships between cosmic bodies, with optional overlays based on the being's position or vibrational alignment.

The UCPS is original in its scope, logic, and visual representation. It is:

  • A tool for positional awareness in the cosmos.
  • A framework for understanding existence as a fractal, evolving system.
  • A bridge between scientific cosmology and spiritual metaphysics.
