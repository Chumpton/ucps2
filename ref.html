The Cosmic Cartesian Coordinates

If the objective of the UCPS is to pinpoint a position within the cosmos, how is it best to express that relationship? We believe it can be elegantly modeled using a simple Cartesian Coordinates system, where each axis represents a primary means of existence within the greater sphere of the cosmos.

❓ Why Are Three Axes Needed to Express the Cosmos?

The cosmos is not a flat plane, nor is existence a single-threaded experience. It is a living tapestry woven through space, time, dimension, and consciousness. To navigate and understand this intricate design, a multidimensional framework is required. Three axes — X, Y, and Z — allow us to plot a soul's precise position within this living matrix.

The distinction can be thought of in this way:

  • X specifies where and when you are — your position in spacetime.
  • Y specifies how you are doing — your vibrational and dimensional expression.
  • Z specifies who you are becoming — your evolving soul essence within the journey of consciousness.

Together, they form a sacred triangulation of identity and presence — grounding the soul's experience across realms, dimensions, and timelines.

  • X Coordinate (Spacetime Position): Captures where and when you exist within the physical and temporal landscape.
  • Y Coordinate (Dimensional Position): Captures the dimensional layer of reality you inhabit, reflecting vibrational and energetic positioning.
  • Z Coordinate (Existential Realms): Captures who you are becoming within the evolutionary journey of consciousness.

Without all three, any map of existence would be incomplete — like trying to locate a star by height alone, or define a being by time without energy or intention.

The Unified Cosmic Positioning System (UCPS) offers a way to honor all aspects of existence simultaneously: matter, energy, and consciousness — body, soul, and spirit — plotted as a dynamic living point within the grand symphony of creation.

Why we need it

UCPS unifies True Date with a three-axis model: X tracks measurable spacetime position and temporal context, Y identifies dimensional and branching reality context, and Z maps consciousness development and existential phase. Together, XYZ form a single coordinate language for where, within which layer, and at what developmental state a point of existence is located.

The X coordinate handles spacetime position with measurable hierarchy: universe, galaxy, star system, planet, satellite, geographic location, and temporal markers. This is the foundation layer, comparable to a cosmic GPS, and answers: where and when is this point located? It supports precision from large-scale cosmic context down to local Earth coordinates and timestamp references.

The Y coordinate handles dimensional context and structural placement. Two entities can occupy the same physical location while existing in different dimensional bands or branching reality pathways. UCPS models this with sub-dimensional zones, reality identifiers, branch context, and vibrational markers. Y answers: within which layer or branch of reality does this point exist?

The Z coordinate handles existential position: realms of consciousness, soul-journey stages, and developmental phase. Where X is location and Y is dimensional structure, Z captures what state of awareness and maturation is active. Together, X, Y, and Z become a modular architecture that can extend into visualization interfaces, simulation design, consciousness research, and educational bridges between scientific and metaphysical domains.

About the Founder: Jen Laird

Jen Laird is the visionary architect and founder of the Unified Cosmic Positioning System (UCPS), a framework born from a lifetime of navigating a world that often felt out of sync. Growing up with a neurodivergent perspective, Jen experienced reality through a unique lens of pattern recognition and hypersensitivity, traits that became the bedrock of her ability to decode the complex geometry of the cosmos.

Her journey was shaped by significant health mysteries that remained unsolved by conventional medicine for years. It was only through her deep, intuitive connection to spirituality and the study of vibrational resonance that she began to find answers. By viewing the human body not just as a biological machine, but as a node within a larger cosmic grid, she was able to resolve her own health crises, leading to the realization that alignment with spacetime coordinates is essential for holistic well-being.

Jen’s background as a pattern-seeker allowed her to synthesize disparate fields, from quantum physics and Cartesian mathematics to ancient spiritual wisdom, into the UCPS. Today, she leads the Cosmic Compass Collective, dedicated to helping others find their own coordinates and navigate their soul’s journey with clarity and purpose.
