## The Cosmic Cartesian Coordinates
The UCPS uses a Cartesian Coordinates (X, Y, Z) system to map a soul's position within the living matrix of the cosmos.

### X Coordinate (Spacetime Position)
*Where and when you are.*
Tracks position through nested cosmic gears:
*   **ULCXx (Universal Life Cycle)**: The universe context (e.g., U0 Observable Universe).
*   **GOx (Galactic Orbit)**: Galaxy's revolution.
*   **GRx (Galactic Rotation)**: Galaxy's spin.
*   **StOx (Stellar Orbit)**: Star's revolution around galactic core.
*   **StRx (Stellar Rotation)**: Star's axial spin.
*   **POx (Planetary Orbit)**: Planet's revolution around star.
*   **PRx (Planetary Rotation)**: Planet's axial spin.
*   **SaOx (Satellite Orbit)** & **SaRx (Satellite Rotation)**.

### Y Coordinate (Dimensional Position)
*How you are doing (vibrational expression).*
*   **D1-D3**: Physical realms (Point, Plane, Volume).
*   **D4**: Time and spatial connection.
*   **D5**: Unity consciousness.
*   **D6-D9+**: Higher dimensional realms (Blueprints, Sound/Light, Archetypes, Source).

**Energetic Signature**: The vibrational "tone" created by the interaction of X, Y, and Z.

### Z Coordinate (Existential Position)
*Who you are becoming.*
Notation: `Z = SJ# / ER#` (Soul Journey Stage / Existential Realm).

*   **Soul Journey Stages (SJ)**: Incarnation, Awakening, Integration, Expansion, Service, Mastery, Ascension.
*   **Realms of Consciousness (RC/ER)**: Physical, Emotional, Mental, Astral, Causal, Etheric, Spiritual, Source.
*   **Existential Phases (E)**: Exploration, Engagement, Mastery, Integration.
